Privacy Policy for New Year Countdown

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android application New Year Countdown (package name com.nightlightstudio.newyearcountdown, hereinafter "the app"), provided by Nightlight Studio ("we", "us"). We respect your privacy and designed the app to work without collecting any personal data.

Summary

  • The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDKs for advertising or profiling.
  • The app does not request any runtime permissions and does not access the internet.
  • All settings you choose are stored locally on your device only.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app has no user accounts, no login and no server component. It does not request or use the INTERNET permission, so no data can leave your device through the app.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your preferences (such as the selected color theme, countdown mode, firework style and sound settings) locally in the app's private storage on your device (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not request any runtime permissions. It runs as a live wallpaper (the Android wallpaper framework requires the BIND_WALLPAPER service permission, which is granted by the system and is not a user data permission). A home-screen widget can be added optionally; it only reads the current date and time on your device to show the countdown.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics services and no other third-party services that collect data.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
